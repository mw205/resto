Platform Overview & Commercial Value for Hospitality
Architecture

Three Intelligent User Experiences .. Functioning as One Cohesive Entity

Engineered to give restaurant operators total mastery over digital sales channels and daily fulfillment:

1. Digital Channel Ownership & Zero Commission

Instead of giving away 15% to 25% commission on every order to third-party delivery apps, Resto grants you a 100% direct digital ordering channel under your brand identity.

2. Operational Fluidity & Real-Time Sync

The instant a guest places an order, it appears in the kitchen queue on the admin console, enabling 1-click driver dispatch without disruptive phone coordination.

3. Customer Data Ownership & Loyalty

Retain full ownership of customer profiles, contact numbers, and order histories to deliver targeted weekly promotional coupons that encourage high-margin reorders.
