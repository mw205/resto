Resto Interactive Demo Guide

Curated 15-Step Journey to Explore the Complete Resto Ecosystem

100% Live Interactive System

How to Experience the Platform as a Restaurant Owner & Investor

This guide takes you through a seamless 5-minute practical journey across the 15 fundamental milestones of the order lifecycle—from customer menu browsing to driver dispatch, real-time tracking, and automated financial tracking in the operations console.

Preconfigured Demo Credentials:
Customer (Mobile App): mohamed@resto.eg
Delivery Driver (Mobile Portal): driver@resto.eg
Operations Manager (Web): admin@resto.eg (+ TOTP)
Recommended 15-Step Journey
Phase 1

Customer Experience (Mobile App)

1

Launch App & Sign In

Log in with mohamed@resto.eg and explore the personalized greeting & weekly promo banner.

2

Browse Menu & Categories

Explore Grills, Clay Pots, and Specialties with calculated calories, prep times, and rich food photography.

3

Add Meal to Shopping Cart

Tap (+ Add) on any dish, adjust quantity, and watch the floating cart counter update instantaneously.

4

Apply Promo Code (RESTO20)

Enter promo code RESTO20 at checkout to witness an instant 20% discount deducted from the subtotal.

5

Confirm Delivery & Cash Payment

Select Home Delivery, verify address details, add a special chef note, and choose Cash on Delivery (COD).

6

Real-Time Order Tracker

View the active order code and Stage 1 highlight ("Order Received & Confirmed") on the visual milestone tracker.

💡 Notice: Rapid, intuitive checkout with zero distracting third-party ads or commission fees.
Phase 2

Operations & Kitchen (Admin Web)

7

Access Web Dashboard

Log into the web console using admin@resto.eg and the on-screen 6-digit TOTP verification code.

8

Review Order in Kitchen Queue

Watch the fresh order appear instantly in the live orders table with ordered items, phone number, and address.

9

Dispatch to Delivery Driver

Assign the order to an available driver (e.g. Captain Hany Saeed) and update status to initiate cooking and dispatch.

Explore in Admin Dashboard:

  • Live revenue analytics and hourly peak order charts.
  • Instant out-of-stock toggle for any menu dish.
  • Driver fleet roster and real-time customer ratings.
💡 Notice: Reactive synchronization propels order updates between web and mobile in sub-seconds.
Phase 3

Delivery & Loyalty (Driver & Reorder)

10

Open Driver Portal

Sign in with driver@resto.eg to view assigned delivery tasks sorted by preparation and delivery urgency.

11

Start Route "Out for Delivery"

Tap start delivery, immediately advancing the customer's live tracker to Stage 3 in real time.

12

Complete Delivery & Collect Cash

Verify the invoice cash amount due and tap the "Delivered to Customer" confirmation button.

13

Check Customer App Status

Observe the customer's tracker switch automatically to "Delivered Successfully" with a completion alert.

14

Rate Meal & Direct Feedback

Submit a star rating or submit an inquiry via the built-in Complaints & Suggestions Center.

15

1-Click Quick Reorder

Open Order History, view past orders, and tap "Reorder" to refill the shopping cart in one second.

💡 Notice: Clear milestone tracking eliminates unnecessary phone inquiries and costly GPS overhead.
✨

Dual-Screen Experience

Open the customer app on your phone alongside the operations web dashboard on your laptop to watch instant live sync across devices.

🛑

Test Out-of-Stock Toggle

Disable any menu dish in the admin dashboard and watch it disappear immediately from the customer app, preventing kitchen bottlenecks.
